## Interpretation

- **Relative strength adds no information here.** Causal relative outperformance at candidate time does not predict
  higher +30-minute returns in the upstream DTU candidate population, in any benchmark frame or with real-time data.
- **It is redundant given the score.**
- **The one robust pattern is about move size, not direction.** The larger the stock's recent relative move, the worse
  the subsequent long return. This matches the PAPER_SIGNAL findings of the same week (continuation-premise failure)
  on a population about seven times larger and upstream of promotion.
- **Cost:** net results are driven further down by event-promoted names' wider spreads.
