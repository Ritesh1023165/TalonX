## What is and is not established

**Established.**
- The replace failed with WinError 5, and the runner had no retry and no handling for it.
- The fixed temp name and unhandled exception left a dead `RUNNING` record.

**Mechanism class**, reproduced on synthetic files only (2026-10-12):
- On this machine, `os.replace` fails with WinError 5 while any process holds the destination open for reading.
  - With a normal Python `open`: reproduced.
  - With `FILE_SHARE_DELETE`: reproduced.
- A tight loop of brief `read_text` calls caused 164 failures out of 300 replacements.
- On-access antivirus and indexer scanning of a just-written file are other known Windows causes that fit the same
  evidence.

**Not established.** Which process held the file at 06:01:33Z. No dashboard or known TalonX reader of `forward_runs/`
existed at the time, and the loop reads the record only after the runner exits. **The holder is unresolved.**

  deadline passed).
