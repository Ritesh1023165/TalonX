WORKSTREAM B — ECONOMIC AND PRODUCT DECISION

B1. State the actual product gap [V2 sparse/negative on configured scope,
broader-panel results don't transfer automatically, Original selective by
design with readiness/edge separate, Experimental mechanics work but no
track record, Intelligence informational not a recommendation, dashboard

B2. Select and execute ONE bounded economic analysis using the existing
product decision and rejection history to choose the most decision-
relevant unresolved analysis supported by available data. Before running,
write a short protocol: exact question and causal rationale; why it is
not a repeat of an already answered hypothesis; dataset/runtime/window;
primary metric, cost convention and exclusions; dependence and
concentration limitations; what conclusion would change the next product
action. Prefer a concrete implementation-versus-economics distinction --
e.g. whether the existing candidate population has negative net outcomes
even before the selective gate, versus whether a verified implementation/
measurement mismatch makes that conclusion unavailable. Do not rerun a
threshold grid merely to produce more trades; do not invent a new signal
family; do not remove losers, expand the live watchlist, or turn a
volatility association into a trading filter. If existing evidence
already answers the candidate analysis, cite it and move directly to the
decision. If essential input is missing, identify it precisely and
complete a bounded reproducible preparation using existing free data
where available -- do not quietly substitute hindsight-selected examples
or incomplete samples.

B3. Separate hypothesis generation from confirmation. Already-inspected
history remains exploratory. State whether genuinely unused data exists;
future live data is not assumed to be the only possible source without
checking the inventory. Report: sample size and effective issuer/time
groups; net economic result and uncertainty; data/execution limitations;
sensitivity only where predeclared; whether the evidence supports any
change.

B4. Produce a decision, not another vague roadmap. Choose one:
1. IMPLEMENT_EXISTING_PRODUCT_GAP -- a specific functional change with
   acceptance criteria.
2. RUN_ONE_JUSTIFIED_EXPERIMENT -- a fixed protocol, available inputs and
   rejection criteria.
3. NO_SUPPORTED_STRATEGY_CHANGE -- explain why and name the smallest
   concrete evidence acquisition needed.
Provide one recommended next task, not a menu of ten. Estimate effort
honestly. Do not promise positive returns or a 50-60% win rate. If no
demonstrated trading edge exists for a requested horizon, say so plainly
while preserving useful informational functionality; do not relabel
informational alerts as BUY/SELL recommendations.
